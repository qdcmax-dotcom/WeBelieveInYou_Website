We Believe in You is dedicated to providing safe, supportive, and structured shared, independent living housing throughout Metropolitan Detroit.

Our homes offer a structured, welcoming environment where participants can rebuild stability, strengthen self-sufficiency, and plan confidently for long-term independence.

Our homes are furnished, maintained, and shared by a group of house mates. Every applicant completes an intake interview, drug and alcohol testing, and review to ensure program fit.

Our Shared Independent Living Programs

Each program participant has their own furnished bed, storage, and access to shared common areas within and just outside of the home.

🏡

Transitional Housing

Our mission is to support individuals and families experiencing housing instability to regain independence, build self-sufficiency, and successfully transition into permanent housing.

  • 📅Short-term and long-term stays available
  • 🤝We work alongside your case manager or care team
  • 📋House rules established to instill responsibility and accountability
  • 💊Medication Assisted Treatment (MAT) Policies as Needed
🌿

Solo Agers

Designed for Solo Agers who want autonomy, safety, and community without relying on family support.

  • 🔑Shared and private living spaces available
  • 👥Built-in community including social activities and peer connections
  • 🚐Limited transportation available for medical appointments for an additional fee
  • 🧹Regular housekeeping services to maintain a hygienic and organized home
